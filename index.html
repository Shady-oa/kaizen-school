About Kaizen Schools

4 Campuses  ★★★★★  Trusted across Kisii County

Kaizen Schools is a private educational institution providing comprehensive early childhood and primary school education across Kisii County.

We are heavily focused on the Competency Based Curriculum (CBC), and widely recognized for our holistic approach to child development — combining strong academics with unique co-curricular activities like coding, robotics, chess, and martial arts.

Our specialized Autism Unit provides a dedicated, safe, and individualized learning facility, because every learner deserves an environment built around how they learn best.
